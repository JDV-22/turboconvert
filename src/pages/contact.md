## Help with a conversion or a bug

Email **[hello@turboconvert.io](mailto:hello@turboconvert.io)** and tell us:

- the tool you used (for example *PDF to Word*),
- your browser and device (for example *Chrome on Windows 11*, *Safari on iPhone*),
- what you expected and what happened instead.

Please **don't attach confidential files**. If a specific file fails, a similar file without personal data helps us reproduce the problem.

## Ideas and missing formats

Missing a tool or a format? Send it to [hello@turboconvert.io](mailto:hello@turboconvert.io). Requests from users decide what we build next.

## Privacy requests

For any question about your personal data or to exercise your rights under the GDPR, write to **[privacy@turboconvert.io](mailto:privacy@turboconvert.io)**. Remember that we never receive the files you convert — see our [privacy policy](/privacy).

## Press and partnerships

Email [hello@turboconvert.io](mailto:hello@turboconvert.io) with "Press" or "Partnership" in the subject line.
