## Why convert MOV to MP3?

MOV is Apple's video format: every iPhone video, every QuickTime screen recording and many camera clips are MOV files. They're also big — a minute of 4K iPhone video takes hundreds of megabytes. When the sound is all you need, an MP3 is a small fraction of that size and plays on any device, including Windows PCs and Android phones that sometimes struggle with MOV.

Typical uses:

- **A voice memo filmed by mistake** — you hit the camera instead of Voice Memos.
- **A rehearsal, a concert or a speech** you recorded on your phone.
- **A Mac screen recording** of a call or a webinar you want to listen to again or transcribe.
- **Interviews and vlogs** where you need the audio track for editing or a podcast.

## Choosing the audio quality

| Audio quality | Size per minute | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| 128 kbps | ≈ 1 MB | Voice, lectures, calls |
| 192 kbps | ≈ 1.4 MB | Everyday listening (default) |
| 256 kbps | ≈ 1.9 MB | Music |
| 320 kbps | ≈ 2.4 MB | Music on good equipment |

## Converting on iPhone, iPad and Mac

**On iPhone or iPad:** open this page in Safari, tap *Choose files*, then *Photo Library* and select your videos. After conversion, the MP3s are in the *Files* app under *Downloads*. Keep the screen on during long conversions — iOS can pause tabs in the background.

**On a Mac:** export the video from Photos (*File → Export → Export Unmodified Original*) or AirDrop it from your iPhone, then drop the .mov file here. Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Edge all work.

## Private by design

Your personal videos stay on your device. Instead of uploading them to a server, this page runs FFmpeg — the open-source engine used by professional video tools — directly in your browser. The engine (about 31 MB) is downloaded once and cached.
