## What you can do with this PDF editor

- **Add text** anywhere: fill in a form that has no fields, annotate a document, correct a date or add a reference number. Change the size and colour (black, blue or red) from the small toolbar above the text.
- **Sign**: draw your signature with a mouse or finger, type it, or upload a photo of it, then place it on as many pages as you need.
- **Add images**: a logo, a stamp, a photo — PNG, JPG or WebP. Resize from the corner handle; proportions are kept.
- **Tick boxes** with a check mark or a cross, and **insert today's date** in one click.
- **White out** a mistake and type over it, or **highlight** a passage in yellow.

## Edit a PDF without uploading it

Most online PDF editors upload your file to their servers, process it there and keep it for a while — and the most capable ones put editing behind a subscription. TurboConvert works differently: the page displays your PDF with PDF.js and writes your changes with pdf-lib, two open-source libraries that run inside your browser. Your document never leaves your device, so you can safely edit contracts, invoices, rental files, tax and administrative forms.

## Changing text that is already in the PDF

A PDF isn't a word-processing document: each word is placed at fixed coordinates, often split into fragments, and the fonts are frequently embedded only partially. That's why even paid editors struggle to rewrite existing text cleanly. The reliable approach, used by most people:

1. Choose **White-out** and click on the word to hide. Drag the corner handle so the white box covers it exactly.
2. Choose **Text**, click on the white box and type the replacement. Adjust the size with the **A−/A+** buttons until it matches.

Remember that white-out hides the old text visually but does not delete it from the file. If you need the document to be fully editable, convert it with [PDF to Word](/pdf-to-word), edit it in Word, then save it back as PDF.

## Tips

- **Click twice on a text** to edit it; drag it to move it. Press <kbd>Delete</kbd> to remove the selected item.
- **To sign several pages**, keep the Signature tool active and click on each page.
- **Need the PDF read-only afterwards?** [Flatten](/flatten-pdf) it, or [protect it with a password](/protect-pdf).
- **Pages in the wrong order or upside down?** Fix them first with [Organize PDF](/organize-pdf) or [Rotate PDF](/rotate-pdf).
