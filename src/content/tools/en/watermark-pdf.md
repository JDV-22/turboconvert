## What a watermark is for

A watermark tells anyone who sees the document what its status is and who it belongs to. Common uses:

- **CONFIDENTIAL / INTERNAL** — board papers, HR documents, financial reports.
- **DRAFT** — versions sent for review, so nobody mistakes them for the final.
- **SAMPLE / COPY / VOID** — documents shared for reference only.
- **"For [name] – [date]"** — the ID card or payslip you send to a landlord or agency. Writing who it is for and why makes a leaked copy much less useful to anyone else.

## Diagonal vs. tiled

| Layout | Looks like | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Diagonal, centered | One large text across the page | Status stamps: DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL |
| Tiled | Text repeated across the whole page | Identity documents, photos, previews you don't want reused |

## Tips

- **Keep it short.** One to three words read best diagonally; longer sentences work better tiled.
- **Watermark copies, not originals.** Keep a clean version for yourself and send the watermarked one.
- **Add page numbers too** with [Add page numbers](/add-page-numbers) for formal documents.
- **Lock it down.** For sensitive files, apply the watermark first and then [password-protect the PDF](/protect-pdf).

Because the watermark is added in your browser, documents such as passports, contracts or unreleased work are never uploaded to a third-party server.

## Watermark your ID before sending it

Landlords, agencies and online services often ask for a copy of your passport, ID card or payslip. A tiled watermark such as *"Rental application – Agency X – Oct 2026"* across the whole page keeps the document readable for its purpose while making a stolen copy far less useful for anything else. Pair it with a moderate opacity (20–30%) so the photo and numbers remain legible.

## Common problems and fixes

**The watermark hides the text.** Lower the opacity to 15–20%. Black text stays readable underneath a light watermark.

**The watermark is barely visible.** Raise the opacity, or use the tiled layout so the text covers more of the page.

**My long text is cut or too small.** A single diagonal line has limited space. Shorten the text, or switch to tiled, which repeats a shorter phrase across the page.

**I need a logo instead of text.** Image watermarks aren't supported. A text watermark with your company name is usually enough to mark ownership.

**I want to remove a watermark.** If you added it here, simply use your original, un-watermarked file — it was never modified.
