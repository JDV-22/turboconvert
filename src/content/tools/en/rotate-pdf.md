## Rotate for good, not just on screen

Most PDF viewers have a rotate button, but it only turns the page on your screen for this session. The next person who opens the file — or you, tomorrow — sees it sideways again. TurboConvert saves the new orientation in the PDF, so it displays correctly in every viewer, on every phone and when printed.

## Which setting fixes my problem?

| Problem | Rotation | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Whole document scanned sideways | 90° clockwise or counter-clockwise | All pages |
| Document upside down | 180° | All pages |
| Every other page upside down after a double-sided scan | 180° | Even pages (or Odd) |
| Landscape tables in a portrait report shown sideways | 90° | Use [Organize PDF](/organize-pdf) to target those pages |

Clockwise means the top of the page moves to the right. If you are unsure, try one direction — converting again from your original file takes a second.

## Tips

- **Mixed problems?** Rotate the bulk of the document here, then fine-tune individual pages in Organize PDF.
- **Scans from a phone** are often sideways because of the camera's orientation; rotate them, then [compress the PDF](/compress-pdf) before sending it.
- **Combining files with different orientations** works fine — [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf) keeps each page's orientation.

Your file stays on your device during the whole process: nothing is uploaded.

## Why pages end up sideways

- **Scanners** feed paper in one direction; a landscape document or a page inserted the wrong way comes out rotated.
- **Phone scans and photos** depend on how the phone was held — and some apps ignore the orientation sensor.
- **Double-sided scanning** of pages bound along the top edge flips every second page upside down.
- **Landscape pages in a portrait document** — spreadsheets and charts inserted into a report — are often shown sideways in viewers.

## Common problems and fixes

**The pages turned the wrong way.** Convert your original again with the opposite direction, or rotate the result by 180°.

**Only some pages needed rotating.** The odd/even option covers duplex scans; for any other selection, use Organize PDF, where every page has its own rotate button.

**The PDF still looks sideways in one app.** Some viewers remember the view you set manually earlier. Close and reopen the file, or reset the view in the app.

**"This PDF is password-protected."** Unlock it first, rotate it, and protect it again if necessary.
