## Remove pages without Acrobat

Deleting a page is one of the most common PDF edits — an unwanted cover, a blank scan page, an annex you don’t need to send — yet free PDF readers usually can’t do it. Adobe Acrobat can, but only in the paid version. TurboConvert shows your pages as thumbnails so you can see exactly what you remove.

## Delete, reorder and rotate in one go

The same screen lets you drag pages into a new order and rotate sideways scans, so one pass fixes the whole document. If you prefer a dedicated tool for that, see [Organize PDF](/organize-pdf).
