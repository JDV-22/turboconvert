## Why convert a JPG to PNG?

JPG is the standard for photos, but it has one weakness: it's lossy, and each time a JPG is edited and saved, the image is compressed again and loses a bit more detail. PNG is lossless, so it's the safer format in a few common situations:

- **Editing in several steps.** Convert once to PNG, then crop, annotate and retouch as often as you like without the image degrading further.
- **Adding text or graphics.** Captions, arrows and logos stay perfectly sharp in PNG, while JPG tends to blur the edges of text.
- **Apps or platforms that require PNG.** Some design tools, app stores, game engines, sticker makers and print templates only accept PNG.
- **Preparing a cut-out.** If you plan to remove the background, starting from PNG lets you save the result with transparency.

## JPG vs PNG

| | JPG | PNG |
|---|---|---|
| Compression | Lossy (adjustable) | Lossless |
| Transparency | No | Yes |
| Size of a typical photo | Small | Large |
| Best for | Photos, sharing | Graphics, screenshots, editing |

For a photo you only want to send or post, keep the JPG — it's already the best format for that. To go back from PNG to a lighter file, use [PNG to JPG](/png-to-jpg); for websites, [JPG to WebP](/jpg-to-webp) gives smaller files than both.

## What happens during conversion

Your browser decodes the JPG, applies its orientation (so photos taken sideways on a phone appear the right way up) and writes the pixels into a PNG at exactly the same width and height. The process runs on your device, takes a fraction of a second per image and works with batches of hundreds of files.

## Other questions

**.jpg** and **.jpeg** are the same format with two spellings, and both are accepted. Camera metadata isn't copied into the PNG, which also removes any GPS location your phone stored in the photo — worth knowing before you publish an image.

**Will converting fix a blurry or pixelated JPG?** No. Blockiness from heavy JPG compression is baked into the pixels; PNG preserves it faithfully. Start from the best original you have.

**Can I convert other formats to PNG?** Yes: use [WebP to PNG](/webp-to-png) or [HEIC to PNG](/heic-to-png).
