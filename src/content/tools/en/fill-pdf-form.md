## Two kinds of PDF forms

**Fillable forms** (also called AcroForms) contain real fields: text boxes, checkboxes, radio buttons and drop-down lists. This page detects them automatically and overlays them in blue — click a field and type, tick boxes, pick options. The values are saved into the fields, exactly as Adobe Reader would do.

**Flat forms** are just pages with lines and boxes: scanned paper forms, or PDFs exported from Word without fields. There's nothing to detect, but you can still fill them: choose **Text**, click at the start of a line and type. Use **Check** or **Cross** for boxes and **Date** to insert today's date.

Both methods can be combined on the same document, and you can **sign** the form before downloading it.

## Tips for a clean result

- **Text too big for the line?** Select it and use **A−** until it fits. The size is kept for the next text you add.
- **Move a text** by dragging it; **edit it** by clicking it twice.
- **Made a mistake on a flat form?** Cover it with **White-out** and type again.
- **Sending the form by email?** If it's too heavy, [compress it](/compress-pdf) after filling.
- **Want to make the answers final?** Run the filled PDF through [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf) so the fields can no longer be edited.

## Your data stays on your device

Administrative and tax forms, job applications, medical questionnaires and rental files are full of personal information. Instead of uploading them to an online service, this page fills the form inside your browser with open-source libraries (PDF.js to display it, pdf-lib to write it). Nothing is uploaded, stored or logged.
