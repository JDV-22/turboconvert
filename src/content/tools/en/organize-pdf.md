## One screen for all page edits

Organize PDF is the visual way to fix a document: instead of typing page numbers, you see the pages themselves. It is the right tool when you need to:

- **Fix the page order** of a scan where sheets went through the feeder in the wrong order.
- **Remove pages** — blank pages, duplicate scans, an unwanted cover or confidential annex.
- **Rotate individual pages** — a landscape table in a portrait report, or one photo taken sideways.
- **Prepare a document before sending** — put the summary first, the appendix last.

## Tips

- **Working with a long scan?** Delete blank pages first; it makes reordering the rest easier.
- **Finish with page numbers.** Once the order is final, [add page numbers](/add-page-numbers) so the printed document is easy to follow.
- **Big file after scanning?** Compress it once all page edits are done.

Your document never leaves your device: thumbnails and the new PDF are both generated locally in your browser.

## A typical clean-up, step by step

1. **Merge** everything that belongs together with [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf) — for example a scanned contract and its annexes.
2. **Open the merged file here** and delete blank pages, duplicates and anything confidential.
3. **Drag pages** into the right order and rotate the ones scanned sideways.
4. **Convert**, then compress the result if it is going by email.

## Common problems and fixes

**The thumbnails take a while to appear.** Each page is drawn as a small image, so a 300-page scan needs some time — especially on a phone. Splitting a very long document into parts makes it quicker to work on.

**I deleted the wrong page.** Your original PDF is never modified. Load it again and start over, or keep working and only click Convert when the order looks right.

**The PDF won't open.** If it asks for a password, unlock it first. If it is damaged, try opening it in your browser's PDF viewer and printing it to a new PDF, then organize that copy.

**Pages appear in a different size.** Organizing keeps each page's own size, so a document that mixes A4 and Letter, or portrait and landscape pages, stays mixed after reordering.
