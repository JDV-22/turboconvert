## Combine PDFs without giving them away

Most online PDF mergers upload your files to their servers, process them there and ask you to trust that they are deleted afterwards. TurboConvert works differently: the merging code is downloaded to your browser and runs on your own device. Nothing is uploaded — you can check it yourself in your browser's developer tools, under the *Network* tab.

That makes it a good fit for documents you would rather not share: signed contracts, invoices, payslips, scanned IDs or school applications.

## What gets kept when you merge

TurboConvert copies every page as-is into a new PDF:

- **Text** stays selectable and searchable.
- **Images and scans** keep their original resolution — no recompression.
- **Page sizes and orientation** are kept, so you can mix A4, Letter and landscape pages.
- **Links inside pages** keep working.

## Tips for a clean result

- **Name your files in order** (for example `01-cover.pdf`, `02-report.pdf`) before selecting them — they will appear in that order, which saves reordering.
- **Mixing scans and digital PDFs?** Merge first, then use [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf) once on the result to get a lighter file for email.
- **Need page numbers across the merged file?** Add them afterwards with [Add page numbers](/add-page-numbers).
