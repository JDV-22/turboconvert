Questions

Is TurboConvert completely free?

Yes. Every tool is 100% free — no hidden fees, no subscription, no account needed. The service is funded by non-intrusive advertising.

Do my files get uploaded to a server?

Never. All conversions run locally in your browser via WebAssembly — no upload, no server processing, nothing to intercept. It's the same level of privacy as desktop software, in a browser tab.

How do I convert a PDF to Word?

Click PDF to Word, upload your file, then click Convert. Your editable DOCX is ready to download in seconds — no account required.

How do I convert MP4 to MP3?

Click MP4 to MP3, upload your video, then click Extract. Your audio file is ready in seconds. Works on any video format including MOV, AVI and MKV.

Are my files safe and private?

Yes. Your files never leave your device. Processing happens 100% in-browser — there is no server involved. Confidential documents, medical files, personal photos — all stay on your machine.

What is the maximum file size?

Most tools support up to 100 MB. Audio and video tools support up to 500 MB — more than enough for the vast majority of use cases.

Do I need to install anything?

No. TurboConvert works entirely in your browser on desktop, tablet or mobile. No installation, no plugin, no signup — just open a tool and convert.

Which file formats are supported?

PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, MP4, MP3, WAV and more across 22 tools. New formats are added regularly.
